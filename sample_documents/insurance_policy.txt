COMPREHENSIVE HEALTH INSURANCE POLICY

Policy Number: INS-2024-001
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Policy Holder: John Doe

SECTION 1: COVERAGE OVERVIEW

This health insurance policy provides comprehensive medical coverage for the insured and eligible dependents. The policy includes coverage for preventive care, emergency services, hospitalization, surgical procedures, prescription medications, and specialized treatments.

SECTION 2: SURGICAL PROCEDURES COVERAGE

2.1 Covered Surgical Procedures
The following surgical procedures are covered under this policy:
- Orthopedic surgeries including knee replacement, hip replacement, and joint repair
- Cardiovascular procedures including bypass surgery and valve repair
- General surgeries including appendectomy, gallbladder removal, and hernia repair
- Emergency surgical procedures performed to save life or prevent serious harm

2.2 Knee Surgery Coverage
Knee surgery is covered under this policy when medically necessary. Covered knee procedures include:
- Arthroscopic knee surgery for meniscus repair
- Knee replacement surgery (partial or total)
- ACL/PCL reconstruction
- Cartilage repair procedures

Conditions for knee surgery coverage:
- Prior authorization required for elective procedures
- Procedure must be deemed medically necessary by a qualified physician
- Conservative treatment must be attempted first unless emergency
- In-network provider preferred for optimal coverage

2.3 Pre-authorization Requirements
All non-emergency surgical procedures require pre-authorization. Failure to obtain pre-authorization may result in reduced benefits or denial of claims.

SECTION 3: BENEFITS AND DEDUCTIBLES

3.1 Annual Deductible: $2,000 per individual, $4,000 per family
3.2 Out-of-pocket Maximum: $8,000 per individual, $16,000 per family
3.3 Coinsurance: 20% after deductible is met
3.4 Copayment: $50 for specialist visits

SECTION 4: EXCLUSIONS

The following are not covered under this policy:
- Cosmetic or elective procedures
- Experimental treatments
- Services obtained outside the United States
- Self-inflicted injuries

SECTION 5: CLAIM PROCEDURES

To file a claim:
1. Obtain prior authorization if required
2. Ensure services are provided by network providers when possible
3. Submit completed claim forms within 180 days of service
4. Include all required documentation

SECTION 6: PRESCRIPTION DRUG COVERAGE

Prescription medications are covered with the following structure:
- Generic drugs: $10 copay
- Brand name drugs: $40 copay
- Specialty drugs: 30% coinsurance

SECTION 7: EMERGENCY SERVICES

Emergency services are covered 24/7 with no prior authorization required. Emergency room visits have a $500 copay which is waived if admitted to hospital.

SECTION 8: PREVENTIVE CARE

Annual preventive care services are covered at 100% with no deductible:
- Annual physical examinations
- Preventive screenings
- Immunizations
- Wellness programs

This policy is governed by the laws of the state where issued and is subject to all applicable federal and state regulations.
